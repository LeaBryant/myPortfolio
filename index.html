01 / WHERE MY STORY STARTS A little context behind the résumé.

Kansas roots.
Arizona raised.
A world to explore.

Before the job titles, there was a girl growing up around sports, family, and the desire to learn.

I was born in Kansas and grew up in Arizona, in a family that loves sports. That part of me didn’t stay in childhood. It followed me into college, into sports media, and into the way I connect with people.

At Grambling State University, I studied multimedia journalism and worked as a football equipment manager. I experienced the stories on the field and the work happening behind the scenes.

Since then, curiosity has taken me through broadcasting, education programs, digital marketing, and technology. These days, the Nashville area is home. The questions, ideas, and love of learning are still coming with me.
