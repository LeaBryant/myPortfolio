MY STORY A little context behind the résumé.

Kansas roots.
Arizona raised.
A world to explore.

Before the job titles, there was a girl growing up around sports, family, and the desire to learn.

I was born in Kansas and grew up in Arizona, in a family that loves sports. At Grambling State University, I studied multimedia journalism and worked as a football equipment manager. Sports became part of both my college experience and my early work in broadcasting.

My career has since taken me through education programs, beauty marketing, and digital content leadership. Along the way, I earned an MBA and learned full-stack web development.

Photography and coding remain creative interests I love exploring. Today, the Nashville area is home, and I’m still finding new things to learn and new ways to tell a story.
