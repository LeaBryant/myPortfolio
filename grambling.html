01 / THE HBCU EXPERIENCE

Black & gold.
Always part of me.

I went to Grambling for an education. I left with experiences, relationships, and a sense of pride that have stayed with me ever since.

I was drawn to sports and storytelling, and Grambling gave me the opportunity to explore both. From the classroom to the football field, my college years were filled with experiences that helped shape who I was becoming.

But my HBCU experience was about more than academics. It was the people, the traditions, the culture, the friendships, and the feeling of belonging to something bigger than myself.

I graduated in 2016 with a degree in Multimedia Journalism, but Grambling gave me much more than a diploma.

02 / THE TEAM BEHIND THE TEAM

My view
of the game.

2013 — 2016

There's a whole team behind the team.

My love for sports opened the door to one of my most meaningful college experiences: working as a football equipment manager.

I helped with equipment, uniforms, inventory, travel preparation, maintenance, and the everyday operations that kept things moving.

It taught me discipline, preparation, teamwork, and the importance of paying attention to details that people don't always see.

Those lessons followed me well beyond the football field.

03 / FINDING MY VOICE

The stories
worth telling.

Journalism gave me a way to turn curiosity into conversations, experiences, and stories.

My time studying multimedia journalism wasn't limited to the classroom. I had opportunities to interview, explore campus events, experience production, and develop a greater appreciation for what goes into telling a story.

05 / FOREVER A TIGER

The chapter ended.
The connection didn't.

Grambling helped me build more than a career foundation. It helped shape my perspective on hard work, opportunity, community, and what it means to show up for something bigger than yourself.

My journey has taken me into different industries, new cities, and opportunities I couldn't have imagined back then.

But the experiences I had at Grambling still show up in the way I work, lead, create, and connect with people.
