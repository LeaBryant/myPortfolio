MORE THAN A LINE ON MY RÉSUMÉ

Black & gold.
Always part of me.

I came to Grambling with a love of sports and an interest in storytelling. Both found a place in my college experience.

I earned my Bachelor of Arts in Multimedia Journalism in 2016 and worked as a football equipment manager from 2013 to 2016. Those experiences brought together the academic and behind-the-scenes sides of my time at Grambling.

Being a proud HBCU alum is part of how I introduce myself.

Football equipment management gave my love of sports a working role.

My responsibilities included equipment and uniforms, inventory, travel preparation, maintenance, and day-to-day operations. It was an experience in the work that helps a team prepare.

That chapter belongs alongside my journalism experience because it helps explain where my interest in sports and the people around the game comes from.
